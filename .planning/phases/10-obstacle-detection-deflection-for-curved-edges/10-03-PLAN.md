<context>
When an obstacle collision is detected by `CurveRouter._detect_collisions`, the router must deflect the curve through clean clearance channels:
1. **Adaptive Waypoint Topology (D-07, D-11):**
   - For compact obstacles ($W_{\text{obs}} \le 2 \times \text{clearance}$): route through a single apex waypoint offset by clearance from the obstacle border, generating $N=2$ segments ($3N+1 = 7$ points).
   - For wide obstacles ($W_{\text{obs}} > 2 \times \text{clearance}$, e.g. $> 30\text{px}$): a single apex clips convex corners (Pitfall 2). Insert 2 corner clearance waypoints $W_1$ and $W_2$ maintaining $\ge 15.0\text{px}$ distance from corners, generating $N=3$ segments ($3N+1 = 10$ points).
2. **Strict $C^1$ Continuity & Tangent Orientation (D-05, D-08):**
   - At each intermediate waypoint $W_i$, orient the tangent vector $\hat{\mathbf{t}}$ parallel to the obstacle border (`Point(1.0, 0.0)` for horizontal bypass).
   - Arrival handle $C_{\text{in}} = W_i - h_{\text{in}} \hat{\mathbf{t}}$ and departure handle $C_{\text{out}} = W_i + h_{\text{out}} \hat{\mathbf{t}}$, satisfying $\mathbf{u}_{\text{out}} = -\mathbf{u}_{\text{in}}$ for smooth, kink-free transitions.
   - The middle segment between $W_1$ and $W_2$ has handles collinear with the corridor line, forming a straight horizontal bypass segment with zero inward bulging.
3. **Corridor Selection & Secondary Avoidance (D-09, D-10):**
   - Evaluate TOP and BOTTOM bypass corridors (or LEFT and RIGHT for vertical flows).
   - Test both candidate splines against all normalized obstacles. If both are collision-free, pick the one with minimal total arc length (minimal deviation heuristic D-09).
   - If secondary obstacles collide, expand corridor outward past secondary obstacles (D-10).
4. **Protocol Badge Centering (D-12):**
   - In `SVGRenderer._render_edges`, decompose $3N+1$ points into $N$ `CubicBezier` segments.
   - Find the segment with maximum `arc_length()` and position the badge at its `arc_length_midpoint(32)` so the label rests on the longest straight bypass chord rather than curved lead-ins.
</context>
